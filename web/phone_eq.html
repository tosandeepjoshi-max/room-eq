Example data: living room, centre seat, measured with REW on 2026-10-03. Add your own recording to replace it.

Measure

  1. Plug the UMIK-1 into the phone. Set the car stereo to a normal listening level.
  2. Put the mic where your head is, pointing at the roof.
  3. Engine off (ignition on), fan off, doors closed.
  4. Live: tap Measure. Recorder app: most recorders pause in the background, so open the recorder and this page in split screen. Start recording, tap Play sweep, wait for Done, stop, then add the WAV. Without split screen, play pocket_sweep_48k.wav from a car USB stick or a music player while the recorder stays in front.
  5. Move the mic 10–20 cm and repeat. 5–7 positions is good.
The live mic is blocked on this page host. Use a recorder app (WAV, 48 kHz, USB mic, no effects) and add the file. The same page hosted on its own HTTPS site can use the mic live.
Recorder app settings
  • Format WAV (PCM), 48 kHz. Mono or stereo is fine.
  • Input: the USB microphone (UMIK-1).
  • Turn off noise reduction, auto gain and any effect.
  • The page finds the sweep inside the recording, so extra seconds before or after are fine.
